きょう波になった話題を、波になった時刻に描いています。

ひとつの話題を、独立した2つ目のソースが取り上げたとき、それは波になります。エコーの見出しはソースの原文のまま表示し、翻訳しません。要約はこのページの言語でモデルが書き、その旨を下に記します。

Display font
Fraunces for Latin letters and every number; Noto Serif SC or Noto Serif JP for Han and kana, weight 900 for headlines and 500 where English uses italic.
Body font
Instrument Sans for Latin; Noto Sans SC or Noto Sans JP for the rest. 16px with line height 1.75 (English is 17px and 1.55).
No italics
Chinese and Japanese have no italic. Kickers and section names use weight 500 instead; fading waves use weight 500 in the secondary ink.
Headline size
Han characters are wider and denser, so the lead is 34 + height ÷ 200 × 34 (English: 44 + height ÷ 200 × 52). Second tier 20 + height ÷ 200 × 16. Third tier 16 + height ÷ 200 × 9.
Line height and spacing
Headlines 1.16 to 1.3, never below. Letter spacing 0; the negative tracking used for English is switched off.
Line breaks
Strict line breaking so punctuation never starts a line; balanced wrapping on headlines. Latin words and numbers inside a headline are not split.
What is translated
Interface text and model summaries. Echo headlines stay exactly as the source published them, in the source language. The wordmark stays “Breaker Delta”; the Chinese page adds 浪尖 beside it and the Japanese page adds 波頭, with its reading なみがしら above it.
Loading
Each language page loads only its own two Noto families, split by character range, so the English page downloads none of them.
