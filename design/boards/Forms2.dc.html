The subscribe form, in six states

A reference sheet, not a page readers reach. The form sits in the footer of every page. Messages appear in place, under the field, and are announced to screen readers. The typed address is never cleared on an error.

1. Resting
Before anything is typed.
One email a day, with the new waves.
2. Sending
From the press until the server answers. The button cannot be pressed twice.
One email a day, with the new waves.
3. Sent
The form is replaced by this message.
One email a day, with the new waves.

Check your inbox. We sent a link to reader@example.com. Open it within 48 hours and the first edition arrives the next morning.

4. Not an email address
Checked when the form is sent, not while typing.
One email a day, with the new waves.

That is not a complete email address. Check the part after the @.

5. Already subscribed
The address is confirmed and active.
One email a day, with the new waves.

reader@example.com already gets the morning edition. Nothing more to do.

6. Could not save
Network or server failure.
One email a day, with the new waves.

We could not save your address. It is still in the box. Try again in a minute.
