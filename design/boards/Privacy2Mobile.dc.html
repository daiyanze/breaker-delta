Privacy.

We keep your email address if you subscribe, and almost nothing else. No ads, no accounts, and no cookies unless you allow them.

Last updated November 20, 2026. Draft wording, to be checked by a lawyer before launch.

What we keep

Very little.

  • Your email address, if you subscribe. With it we keep the edition you chose (daily or weekly), the language, and the dates you subscribed and confirmed.
  • Your theme choice (light or dark). It is stored in your browser, not on our servers.
  • Visit counts. Umami and Plausible count page views, the page a visitor came from, the country and the kind of device. They set no cookies and do not follow anyone from one visit to the next. If you allow it, Google Analytics counts visits too; it does set cookies.
  • Server logs: the address a request came from, the page, and the time. They are used to keep the site running and to stop abuse.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no advertising trackers.
  • No cookies at all, unless you say yes to Google Analytics.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of email addresses.
  • No account, so no password and no profile.

Cookies

The site sets no cookies by default. Google Analytics is the only thing here that uses them, and it loads only after you choose Allow in the bar at the bottom of the page. Choose No thanks and it never loads; the site works exactly the same.

Your answer is remembered in your browser for 6 months. You can change it at any time with the Cookie choice link at the bottom of every page.

If your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal, Google Analytics stays off and we do not ask. Umami and Plausible, which set no cookies, still count the visit.

How long we keep it

Your email address stays until you unsubscribe. After that it is deleted within 30 days. An address that was never confirmed is deleted after 48 hours. Server logs are deleted after 14 days.

Your choices

Every email has a link to unsubscribe and a link to change how often you hear from us. You can also ask us to delete or send you what we hold about you; write to privacy@breakerdelta.com.

Who else handles it

The site runs on Cloudflare, and emails are sent through an email delivery service, and visits are counted by Umami, Plausible and, only with your consent, Google Analytics. They process the data above for us and for no purpose of their own.

Copies run by other people

Breaker Delta is open source. Anyone can run their own copy. This page covers breakerdelta.com only; a copy at another address is run by someone else, under their own rules.

Changes

If this page changes in a way that matters, subscribers hear about it by email before it takes effect.
