Write an email: the other three templates

A reference sheet. Board 49 shows the form with Notice chosen. Choosing another template swaps only the fields below; “To”, the language tabs, the formatting bar, the live preview and the two Send buttons stay as on board 49.

Correction
An email or a page of ours was wrong.
“We wrote” and “It should say” cannot be left empty: Send stays off until both are filled, and the message under the field says which one is missing. “We wrote” is shown struck through in the email. If “What was wrong” is an edition, the email goes only to the people who received that edition.
Announcement
Something new, or something changing.
The same fields as Notice. The only difference is in the email: a blue “New at Breaker Delta” label in place of the yellow one, and Breaker carrying a letter.
Letter
Words only, in the desk’s own voice.
No headline, no label and no button. Delta the cat sits beside the sign-off. A letter is the only template where the first line the reader sees is the greeting.
