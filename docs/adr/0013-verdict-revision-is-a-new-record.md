# 0013 A confirmed verdict is revised by a new record

Date: 2026-11-21. Status: accepted. Refines "a confirmed verdict does not
change".

## Decision

A confirmed verdict row is never edited. If the editor later rules
differently, that is a new verdict record with a reason. The track record and
the topic page show the latest verdict, keep the original visible and struck
through, and mark the wave as revised.

## Why

Evidence can arrive after day 14. Forbidding revision would leave known-wrong
verdicts standing; editing in place would let the record be improved quietly.
Appending keeps both honest.

## Consequence

The prediction itself stays immutable (invariant 5).
