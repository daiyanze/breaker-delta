# 0006 Append-only content with visible corrections

Date: 2026-11-18. Status: accepted.

## Decision

Published summaries are never edited in place. A rewrite is a new version.
Corrections and withdrawals are new records shown to readers. Withdrawing a
summary does not remove its review registration.

Unattended publishing has a fact gate: a summary flagged high-risk (named
person or company; appointment, dismissal, lawsuit, accident, casualty) with
fewer than two first-hand sources is published as "unverified" in neutral
wording and kept out of the email digest.

## Why

The track record is the product's credibility. It must not be possible to
improve it by deleting things, and a wrong claim must be removable within
minutes without rewriting history.
